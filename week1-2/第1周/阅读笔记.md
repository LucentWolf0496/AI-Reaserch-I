1.用自己的话解释“用户只控制二维位置，为什么机器人仍可以完成三位抓放”：
用户控制的二维位置机械臂移动是一个粗略的探路，是抓取步骤较简单的部分，只需要大致的位置移动。
由于本课题原论文旨在帮助残疾人员完成精细抓取，故重在实现copilot的三维精细操作，因为这是残疾人员无法精细完成的。
而二维移动正常或残疾均可完成，故copilot会自动在二维位置CV模块检测完成后自动完成三维的手指层级精细操作。
这就是二维简单步骤+三维复杂操作的联合搭配，使得用户和机器人协同解决取物问题。


2.论文初步阅读笔记
Goal: AI copilot helps improving BCI’s working performance.
Experiment Method: Comparison between healthy participants and disabled ones & with AI-copilots open or closed.

Strategy: 
1-	CNN&KF structure-decoding electroencephalography.
Fine-tuning: intention-oriented training process to enable adaptation.
It is intention that determine the movement, but not the eye. Intention-oriented training process aims to decrease the influence from eye movement.
Researchers want to cut down the disturbance of eye-moving information. Through a specially designed training paradigm, they decorrelate motor intention from eye movement, so that the CNN learns to ignore eye-related signals. 
After such training and later analysis of the hidden layers of CNN, it turns out that CNN doesn’t include information about eye moving anymore. Therefore, researchers finally freeze CNN to guarantee that eye-moving information is excluded, while KF part is still available to adapt to other factors.
2-	Copilot demonstration-RL for cursor control and CV for robotic arm control.
*** They are two crucial parts of this experiment: 
*** A movement combines task determination completed by CV part and cursor moving.
*** Copilots are designed to improve their performance.
The copilot is not meant to replace the user. It is meant to infer the user’s goal and aid completion.

Detailed information:
The final effect of experiments:
Participants are able to use a copilot to autonomously and proficiently perform a sequential pick-and-place task with a non-invasive EEG AI-BCI.

In which aspects can AI copilots improve participants’ movement?
Including: enhance velocity, enhance path efficiency, minimize dial-in time, increase hit rate and success rate
Conclusion: infer user goals and aid their completion

How the training process works for such effects?
Offline pretraining
(CNN&KF: decoding EEG signals & CV: determining tasks & RL: copilot)
Subject-specific fine-tuning
(intention-oriented complex labels)
Closed-loop co-adaptive training
(Build up the collaborative bounds between participants and copilots, deciding the support that copilots give to participants. The participation of copilots is influenced by the familiarity of the participant’s operation during the whole process – via PPO RL learning)

Further potential improvement:
1.	Increase EEG robustness by reducing pain and paraesthesia
2.	Reduce the DOF(degrees of freedom) required to handle objects
3.	Enable advanced robots to be copilots, enabling AI-BCIs to handle even more complex movement tasks.
